Place optional clan logos in this folder. The filename (without its extension)
must match the clan name. Matching is case-insensitive.

Current filenames:
  OFIN.png
  HG.png
  KRTS.png
  7DR.png
  RMC.png
  48th.png
  7PD.png
  ZFG.png
  PG60.png
  7CIE.png

PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP files are supported. Transparent square PNG images
are recommended. Missing or unreadable logos are skipped without preventing
the leaderboard from rendering.
